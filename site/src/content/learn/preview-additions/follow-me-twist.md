## Twist and end scale

On an **open** path, Follow Me can turn the profile as it travels and grow or shrink it toward the far end, for a twisted bar, a fan blade, or a tapered horn. Before you finish the sweep, type a value and press `Enter`:

- `30deg` twists the profile 30° over the whole path (`30d` and `30°` work too). A bare `30` is a length, for a partial sweep.
- `40%` makes the profile 40% of its size at the far end.
- `8s` sets how many slices the sweep uses between corners. Hew picks a sensible number on its own.

You can type these at the profile step, where the status bar reminds you of them, or while you drag a partial sweep. The status bar shows what's set, such as "(Twist 30° · End 40%)". The settings reset for each sweep, and typing a new value right after you finish redoes the sweep. A loop can't twist and still meet itself, so a full sweep of a closed path with a twist or end scale says "Twist and end scale need an open path." A partial sweep of a loop is fine.
