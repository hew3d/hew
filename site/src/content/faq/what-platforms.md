---
question: "What platforms does Hew run on?"
order: 5
---

Hew runs as a native app on macOS, Windows, and Linux, and as a web app at [app.hew3d.com](https://app.hew3d.com) in any modern, WebGL2-capable browser. It also runs as a "PWA" on your phone, in "Shop Mode" (read-only and laser-focused on measurement). Finally, you can self-host the web app locally, from a simple nginx drop-in all the way to a full Proxmox LXC setup.
