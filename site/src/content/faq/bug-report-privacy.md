---
question: "What happens when I report a bug?"
order: 9
---

Help ▸ Report Bug… opens a dialog showing exactly what would be sent — your description, and a checklist of app/system details, recorded steps, imported files, your model, and a log tail, each with its size and a preview. Imported files are sent byte for byte: the files you imported or opened, and the textures and library items you added. If a file wouldn't open, the checklist also offers that file, byte for byte. On the desktop app, your home folder's path is replaced with `~` in the log tail before you see the preview, and a row of raw input events is listed only when Debug Mode is on. Nothing leaves your machine until you click Send report, and it goes to a private intake service on Hew's account, not a public tracker. You get a report ID back, and can optionally open a public GitHub issue from there too, with no attachment. See the [bug report privacy page](/privacy/bug-reports/) for what's stored, who reads it, and how long it's kept. If you leave the "Let me know what happens with this report" box ticked, Hew keeps a private key on your computer to check for replies, and the [following section](/privacy/bug-reports/#following-a-report) of the privacy page says exactly what is stored and when Hew checks.
