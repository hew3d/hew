---
question: "Is Hew free and Open Source?"
order: 2
---

Yes. Hew is licensed under the AGPL-3.0, so it is fully Open Source and free — no subscription, no account, no feature tier held back for a paid plan. The source lives at [github.com/hew3d/hew](https://github.com/hew3d/hew). It will be *possible* to create proprietary plugins in the future, but Hew itself will always be completely free.
